How it works

Three steps to zero voicemail backlogs

VoiceCare Pro plugs into your existing softphone in minutes — no hardware changes, no IT project.

01

Forward your voicemails

Your clinic gets a unique email address like np1psy@mail.getvoicecarepro.com. Configure your softphone (RingCentral, VoIP, etc.) to forward audio files there.

→
02

AI does the heavy lifting

Our AI transcribes the audio, extracts the patient name, callback number, determines the right department, and writes a clear recommended action — in seconds.

→
03

Act, don't listen

Your staff receives a clean email summary for every voicemail, plus a full dashboard to track status, filter by department, and monitor analytics.
