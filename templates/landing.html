How It Works

From voicemail to action in 3 steps.

No new hardware. No workflow disruption. Your softphone stays exactly the same.

01
📲

Forward Your Voicemails

We give your clinic a unique email address like [email protected]. Set your RingCentral or any softphone to forward voicemails there — done.

🤖
02

AI Triages Instantly

Our AI listens to the audio, extracts the caller's name, call-back number, intent, and department — stripping away everything irrelevant in seconds.

📧
03

Your Staff Gets the Summary

A clean, structured email is sent from [email protected] with exactly what's needed — no audio file, no reading transcripts.

📊
04

Track & Manage in Dashboard

View all voicemails, filter by department, listen to original audio, read full transcripts, and track status — all in one place.

Features

Everything clinical staff actually need.

Built specifically for the pace and complexity of healthcare front-desk operations.

  • 🎯

    Intent Extraction

    AI identifies the core reason for the call — appointment request, billing inquiry, prescription, and more — filtering out all filler conversation.

  • 📞

    Dual Phone Capture

    Automatically captures both the caller ID number and any callback number the patient mentions verbally during the voicemail.

  • 🏥

    Department Routing

    AI detects and suggests the correct department — Front Desk, Billing, Nursing, Lab — so the right team gets notified immediately.

  • ⚡

    Recommended Action

    Don't just tell staff what was said — tell them exactly what to do next, with the callback number included in the action prompt.

Refund Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025

Monthly Subscriptions

All monthly subscription fees are non-refundable. If you cancel your subscription, you will retain access to the Service until the end of your current billing period. No partial-month refunds will be issued.

Exceptions

We will issue a full refund for the most recent billing period if: (a) you experienced a material Service outage lasting more than 48 consecutive hours that was caused by VoiceCare Pro and was not due to circumstances beyond our control; or (b) you were charged incorrectly due to a billing error on our part. Refund requests must be submitted within 14 days of the charge in question.

Free Trial

If a free trial is offered, you will not be charged until the trial period ends. You may cancel before the trial ends to avoid any charges.
