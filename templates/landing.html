HIPAA Compliance Notice

Last updated: January 1, 2025

Our Role Under HIPAA

VoiceCare Pro recognizes that healthcare providers using our platform may transmit voicemails containing Protected Health Information (PHI) as defined under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (HIPAA) and its implementing regulations. In such cases, VoiceCare Pro acts as a Business Associate to your Covered Entity.

Business Associate Agreement (BAA)

A Business Associate Agreement is available to all VoiceCare Pro customers whose use of the Service involves PHI. To request a BAA, contact us at [email protected]. You should not use VoiceCare Pro to process PHI until a BAA is executed between your organization and VoiceCare Pro.

Safeguards We Implement

  • Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) for all data transmissions
  • Encryption at rest for stored voicemail audio and transcriptions
  • Role-based access controls limiting staff access to relevant data
  • Audit logs for data access and system events
  • Automatic data deletion protocols following account termination
  • Employee training on data privacy and security

Minimum Necessary Standard

VoiceCare Pro's AI is designed to extract only the minimum information necessary to produce actionable summaries. We do not store or transmit PHI beyond what is required to deliver the Service.

Breach Notification

In the event of a discovered breach of unsecured PHI, VoiceCare Pro will notify affected Covered Entities in accordance with the HIPAA Breach Notification Rule (45 CFR Part 164, Subpart D) within the timeframes required by law.
